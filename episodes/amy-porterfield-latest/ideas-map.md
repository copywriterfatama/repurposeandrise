# Ideas Map — The Amy Porterfield Show: "The Money Already Hiding in Your Business"

**Transcript source:** uploaded by Fatama (YouTube caption export with chapter headings, no
timecodes — source locators below reference chapter titles). Filed at
`episodes/amy-porterfield-latest/transcript/transcript.txt`.
**Governing method:** Gap × Layer Matrix (per Fatama's uploaded Content Audit Framework —
see `docs/Content_Audit_Framework.md`). Flag: confirm this is ARC10 / the intended method.
**Client goal / CTA (assumed — confirm):** drive sign-ups to Amy's free live training for
multi-6-figure female founders at amyporterfield.com/freetraining. This is the episode's own
stated CTA ("find your marketing blind spot"); no other goal was specified.

**Episode through-line:** When revenue feels shaky, founders chase new leads. The 90-day
question — "if you couldn't get a single new lead for 90 days, what would you do?" — forces
the inward look, and five real founder examples show the money already sitting in the
business: alumni offers, neglected offers, evergreen re-promotion, churn, conversion rate.

**Gap coverage note (drives selection):** the episode is unusually rich in MISCONCEPTION
(revenue problem ≠ traffic problem), KNOWLEDGE GAP (the five levers, the funnel drop-off
diagnostic), and PROOF (three specific dollar-figure stories: $10K/mo churn, $100K conversion,
60-70% vs 5-20%). It is light on IDENTITY and PERMISSION. Selection below leans into the
proof and reframe material because both ladder directly into a diagnostic-flavored CTA
("find your blind spot") — the training is the natural next step after each reframe.

## 1. INSTAGRAM (primary — deepest)

### IG-01 · Reel (hook = her own question, near-verbatim)
- **Core idea:** If you couldn't get a single new lead for 90 days, how would you make money?
- **Angle:** The episode's diagnostic question is a natural scroll-stopper — it creates an
  instant, personal thought experiment the viewer can't not answer. Belief · Layer 4
  (reframe), TOFU→MOFU bridge.
- **Goal connection:** The question IS the training's pitch in miniature (inward diagnostic →
  blind spot). Direct hand-off to the free training CTA.
- **Source:** "The 90-Day Question That Exposes Hidden Revenue"

### IG-02 · Carousel (the episode's full value, save-worthy)
- **Core idea:** 5 places money is already hiding in your business — no new leads required.
- **Angle:** One slide per opportunity (alumni offer · neglected offer · evergreen re-promo ·
  churn · conversion rate). Checklist energy = saves and shares. Knowledge Gap · Layers 2–4,
  MOFU.
- **Goal connection:** Demonstrates the depth behind the training; the "these are 5 of many —
  the training finds YOUR blind spot" close is built into the material.
- **Source:** all five "Opportunity" chapters

### IG-03 · Quote/stat post
- **Core idea:** You have a 60–70% chance of selling to an existing customer — and 5–20% to
  a stranger. You're marketing to the wrong list.
- **Angle:** Hard-number misconception-buster; the gap between the two stats does all the
  work. Misconception · Layer 4, MOFU.
- **Goal connection:** Shifts belief from "get more leads" to "look inward" — the exact
  belief the training monetizes.
- **Source:** "Opportunity 1: Sell to the Buyers You've Ignored"

### IG-04 · Reel or carousel (story-led, real numbers)
- **Core idea:** She dropped her churn 2 percentage points — and found $10,000/month she was
  losing through the back door.
- **Angle:** Specific member story with a DM'd punchline ("she went home and ran the
  numbers"). Retention framed as the invisible lever you only notice when it's gone.
  Proof · Layer 10 + Pain · Layer 3 (hidden cost), MOFU→BOFU.
- **Goal connection:** "Know your numbers" is the training's core behavior; story ends on
  the diagnostic invitation.
- **Source:** "Opportunity 4: Plug the Churn Leak in Your Membership"

### IG-05 · Reel (story-led, biggest number in the episode)
- **Core idea:** A 1.5% conversion bump on a funnel she'd already built = $100,000/year. No
  new traffic. No new offer.
- **Angle:** The conversion rate itself is the lever — the money was in mapping what fixing
  it was worth. Proof · Layer 10 + Decision Trigger · Layer 8, MOFU→BOFU.
- **Goal connection:** Blind-spot-in-dollars, the strongest single proof point for "the

- **Source:** "Opportunity 5: The Conversion Tweak Worth $100K"

### IG-06 · Carousel (practical diagnostic)

- **Angle:** Opt-in → opens → clicks → show-up → pitch; weak opt-in = lead magnet, low

### IG-08 · Reel

## Selection reasoning — top 5 picks (Gap × Layer logic)

1. **IG-01** — The 90-day question is the sharpest asset in the episode: a Layer 4 reframe
   compressed into one sentence, in Amy's own words, that filters for exactly the founder
   the CTA serves. Highest hook-strength-to-effort ratio on the map.
2. **IG-05 / SH-01** — $100K from a 1.5% bump is the episode's best Proof · Layer 10
   material: specific, verifiable-feeling, and it monetizes the blind-spot concept — the
   training's promise in dollar form.
3. **IG-04** — The churn story adds a second independent proof point with a different lever
   and a human arc (event → went home → ran numbers → DM). Two proof stories beat one
   repeated.
4. **IG-02** — The five-opportunity carousel is the save/share vehicle: it carries the whole
   episode's utility and earns list-adjacent behavior (saves signal the algorithm; the close
   seeds the CTA).
5. **LI-02** — "Revenue problems are rarely traffic problems" is the master misconception of
   the episode and the load-bearing belief-shift for the entire funnel; LinkedIn is where
   that argument reads as authority rather than hot take.

**What was cut and why:** the "study 3 competitors' webinars" aside (useful but sends
attention outward — off-thesis), the "use Claude to analyze" mention (dates the content,
serves no gap), and a possible "sometimes it IS a traffic problem" nuance post (honest but
dilutes the reframe at this stage of the funnel).
