# Gap × Layer Matrix — adapted for episode-level idea selection

Source: Fatama's Content Audit Framework (companion to the Gap × Layer Matrix). The audit
version diagnoses a finished post; this adaptation runs the same lens in reverse — deciding
which ideas inside an episode deserve to become posts.

## The 10 Gaps (what a piece of content can close for the audience)

| # | Gap | What it addresses |
|---|-----|-------------------|
| 1 | **Pain** | The problem the audience is actively struggling with |
| 2 | **Desire** | What they actually want (articulated and unarticulated) |
| 3 | **Belief** | What they currently believe — helping or hurting them |
| 4 | **Objection** | Why they hesitate, even when they want what's offered |
| 5 | **Misconception** | The myths and false assumptions keeping them stuck |
| 6 | **Knowledge Gap** | The things they don't know they need to know |
| 7 | **Identity** | Who they are, who they're becoming, who they're rejecting |
| 8 | **Proof** | The evidence they need to believe it's possible for them |
| 9 | **Permission** | The internal blocks that need releasing before they can act |
| 10 | **Decision Trigger** | The signal, moment, or condition that moves them to act |

## The 10 Layers (depth, mapped to funnel stage)

| Layer | Funnel | Reader state |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | TOFU | Recognition / scroll-stopping |
| 2 | TOFU | Naming what was unspoken |
| 3 | TOFU→MOFU | Hidden depth / "oh, that's me too" |
| 4 | MOFU | Reframing / "wait, I've been thinking about this wrong" |
| 5 | MOFU | Deeper resonance / "how do they know me?" |
| 6 | MOFU | Pattern naming / removing self-blame |
| 7 | MOFU→BOFU | Cost / consequence / loss aversion |
| 8 | MOFU→BOFU | Tipping point / readiness signal |
| 9 | BOFU | Permission / decision-clearing |
| 10 | BOFU | Story / proof / closing the loop |

## Applying it in Phase 1

**Scan first, select second.** Before extracting ideas, mark each gap's coverage in the
episode: HEAVY · MODERATE · LIGHT · ABSENT. Episodes are lopsided — a teaching episode is
usually rich in Knowledge Gap and Misconception; a story episode in Proof and Identity.
Selection should lean into the heavy gaps: that's where the episode's native power is.
Don't force ideas out of LIGHT/ABSENT gaps just for coverage — thin source material makes
generic posts.

**Strong ideas hit 1-3 gaps at 1-3 layers.** An idea that "covers everything" is noise; an
idea that targets nothing specific is decoration. When tagging an idea (e.g. "Proof ·
Layer 10 + Pain · Layer 3"), the tag should describe what the finished post will *do* to
the reader, not merely what topic it mentions.

**Match layer to funnel job.** The map as a whole should spread across the funnel:
- Quote posts and one-line reframes → Layers 1-2 (TOFU reach)
- Checklists, numbered breakdowns, diagnostics → Layers 3-6 (MOFU saves/shares)
- Dollar-figure stories, case proof, cost-of-inaction → Layers 7-10 (MOFU→BOFU conversion)

**Proof material is gold.** Specific numbers, named results, and complete story arcs in the
speaker's own words are the rarest and highest-converting material in any episode
(Layer 10). If the episode contains two or more independent proof stories, keep them as
separate ideas — two proof stories beat one repeated.

**Every idea ladders to the client goal.** The Gap × Layer tag explains why the idea works
on a reader; the goal connection explains why it's worth the client's feed space. An idea
that can't articulate its strategic job gets cut, no matter how clever.
