## 3) ICP (who I sell to)

**The content-rich, leverage-poor expert** — established coach/consultant/course creator who:
- Has a podcast or YouTube show and a **1K–50K** audience.
- Already has an offer and makes money (has budget + reason to want more leads).
- Pours hours into long-form, then watches it die after one listen.
- Feels permanently behind on "showing up everywhere."
- Has tried: doing it themselves (unsustainable), a cheap VA (needed too much hand-holding),
  or batching (fell off).
- **Skews female** (coaches/creators) — design and copy for a sophisticated female buyer.

**Their internal monologue:** *"I put everything into these episodes and then nothing. I know
I should post everywhere. I don't have the time. And people with worse content are more visible
than me."*

**NOT for:** no long-form to repurpose; pre-revenue with no offer; chasing virality over leads.

## 4) POSITIONING & THE CORE REFRAME (the hinge of all messaging)

They believe it's a **time problem** ("if I had more hours, I'd post more"). Move them to see
it as a **leverage problem**: *"You don't need to create more; you need to extract more from
what you already made. One episode isn't one piece of content — it's the raw material for thirty.
Your best thinking already happened, on the mic. The work isn't making more — it's finishing
what you started."*

**Positioning pillars:** Leverage not volume · Judgment not clipping (why cheap VAs/AI make
garbage) · The Invisible Load Index™ (name the hidden mental cost, then relieve it) · Proof over
promises (lead with Lu Levy's numbers).

**Signature hooks:** "You spent two hours on that episode. It got heard once, then it died." ·
"You don't have a content problem. You have a leverage problem." · "One episode = 30 days of
content." · "Repurposing isn't more content. It's finishing the content you already made."
